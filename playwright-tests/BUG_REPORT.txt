SU-001: SECURITY ISSUE - Non-Canadian Phone Numbers Accepted
----------------------------------------------------------
Severity: CRITICAL
Area: Signup Validation
Status: ACTIVE

Description:
The signup form accepts non-Canadian phone numbers and successfully creates accounts, which violates business requirements that only Canadian phone numbers should be allowed.

Actual Behavior:

Impact:
